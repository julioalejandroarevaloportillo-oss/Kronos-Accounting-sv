# 📋 SKILL 04: Eisenhower Quadrants & Deep-Work Timeboxing

## 🎯 Purpose
Filter tasks, eliminate fake work, and schedule critical high-impact priorities into uninterrupted focused blocks.

## 🛠️ Execution Protocol
1. **Classify by Quadrant:**
   - **Q1 (Urgent & Important):** Crisis, hard tax/legal deadlines $\rightarrow$ Do now.
   - **Q2 (Not Urgent & Important):** Systems design, automation, test coverage $\rightarrow$ Schedule deep work.
   - **Q3 (Urgent & Not Important):** Routine notifications, trivial pings $\rightarrow$ Delegate or automate.
   - **Q4 (Not Urgent & Not Important):** Vanity tweaks, aimless browsing $\rightarrow$ Delete.
2. **Timeboxing Architecture:** Allocate 90-minute high-focus sprints separated by 15-minute recovery intervals.
3. **WIP Limits:** Restrict Work-In-Progress to a strict limit of 3 concurrent active tasks.
